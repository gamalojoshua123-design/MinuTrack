What does "Delivery" mean here?

This affects how we track delivery times and status on the dashboard

A

Deliveries TO the Branch

Inventory/stock shipments from suppliers arriving at the branch. Track when supplies were ordered, shipped, and received. Measures how fast the branch gets restocked.

B

Deliveries FROM the Branch

Customer orders being delivered out to customers. Track order placed → prepared → dispatched → delivered. Measures customer delivery speed.

C

Both Directions

Track both incoming supplier deliveries AND outgoing customer deliveries. Full logistics visibility for the branch owner.
